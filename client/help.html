Frequently Asked Questions

How does insurance work?

ToolShare provides basic coverage for tools listed on the platform. If a tool is damaged during a rental, you can file a claim through our protection program. We verify all borrowers to ensure your tools are in safe hands.

What if the tool isn't returned?

We hold a security deposit for every booking. If a tool isn't returned, we use the deposit to compensate the owner. We also have a dedicated recovery team to handle such rare cases.

How do I get paid?

Earnings are automatically credited to your ToolShare wallet after a booking is successfully completed. You can withdraw your balance to your bank account via UPI or direct transfer within 24 hours.

Are there any listing fees?

No, listing tools on ToolShare is completely free! We only take a small service fee (10%) from the total booking amount to cover platform maintenance and insurance.

Still have questions?

Our support team is available 24/7 to help you with any issues.
