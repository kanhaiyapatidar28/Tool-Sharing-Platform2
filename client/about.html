Building the Neighborhood
Tool Shed

ToolShare is India's first peer-to-peer tool rental marketplace, designed to help neighborhoods share resources, reduce waste, and build community.

Our Mission

We believe that you shouldn't have to buy a drill just to hang one picture. Most power tools are used for less than 15 minutes in their entire lifetime. We're here to change that.

By connecting tool owners with neighbors who need them, we create a more sustainable way to get jobs done while putting extra cash in owners' pockets.
