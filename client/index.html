Contact Support
Is it free to join and list tools? +
Yes, completely free. There are no listing fees or membership costs. We take a small 10% commission only when a rental is completed.
What if the tool gets damaged? +
Every rental is protected by our Damage Protection Plan. Borrowers pay a refundable security deposit, and our dispute resolution team handles any claims within 48 hours.
How do I verify my identity? +
We require a government-issued ID (Aadhaar or PAN) during onboarding. Verification takes under 5 minutes and is required before your first booking.
How quickly do owners get paid? +
Payouts are processed within 24 hours of a rental completion directly to your registered bank account or UPI ID.
Can I rent tools for multiple days? +
Absolutely. You can set rental duration by the hour, day, or week. Most owners offer discounted weekly rates — check the listing for details.
